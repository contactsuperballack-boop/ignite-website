Cookie Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Short version: this site does not set tracking or advertising cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. They are used to remember settings, keep you signed in, or measure and track activity.

What we use

  • Our own cookies: none. The site does not set cookies for tracking, advertising or personalisation, and it has no sign-in.
  • Analytics: if enabled, Vercel Web Analytics measures page views and button clicks without using cookies to identify you.
  • Videos: portfolio videos are hosted on this site and load only when you press play. They do not use embedded players from other companies.

Third-party resources

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and an animation library from the cdnjs network. These providers receive technical information such as your IP address, and to our knowledge they do not set cookies on our pages.

Links to other sites

If you follow links to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp, those services may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because we do not set non-essential cookies, you will not see a cookie banner here.

Changes

If we add analytics cookies, embedded media or advertising tools, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required. Questions: contact.superballack@gmail.com.
