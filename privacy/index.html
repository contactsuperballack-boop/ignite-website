Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information Ignite collects through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Ignite (“we”, “us”) is an SI-native company that works remotely and is based in Africa. Ignite is part of Trustbridge Corp. You can reach us at contact.superballack@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use an inquiry or waitlist form, the site prepares a WhatsApp or email message for you. It can include your name, email address, organisation, phone or WhatsApp number, timeline, area of interest and the details you write. This reaches us only when you send that message. The website itself does not store form entries.
  • Usage measurements. If enabled, we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and button clicks (for example, which contact option is used). It records things like the page visited, referring site, approximate country, and device and browser type. It does not use cookies to identify you.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical data such as your IP address and request details to deliver the site and keep it secure.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss or deliver services.
  • To manage your place on the Ignite Academy waitlist and contact you about it.
  • To understand which pages and contact options work, and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and meet legal obligations.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we rely on your consent, steps needed to respond to your request, and our legitimate interests in running and securing the site.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We use service providers who process data for us or when you contact us: Vercel (hosting and analytics), WhatsApp (Meta) and your email provider when you send us a message. Fonts load from Google Fonts and an animation library loads from the cdnjs network, so those providers receive your IP address when a page loads.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside your country. We choose reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information when it is transferred.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and to keep ordinary business records. Waitlist details are kept until you ask us to remove them or we no longer need them. Analytics data is kept by Vercel under its own retention practices.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, and ask for a copy in a portable form. Email us at contact.superballack@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security and children

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service is completely secure. The site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has sent us information, tell us and we will remove it.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
